# Market Segmentation

## Required Dimensions

| Dimension | SaaS / B2B Examples | Why It Matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Country / Region | US, UK, CA, AU, EU, emerging markets | ROI 和付费能力常按市场分化 |
| Language | English, Spanish, German | 文案和落地页匹配 |
| Intent | problem, solution, competitor, template, free, tutorial | 解释 query/audience 质量 |
| Funnel Stage | awareness, consideration, high intent, remarketing | 防止预算混投 |
| Company Fit | individual, SMB, mid-market, enterprise | 影响 trial-to-payment 和 AOV |
| Offer | free trial, demo, template, discount, consult | 影响注册质量 |
| Device | desktop, mobile, tablet | B2B trial/付费可能设备差异明显 |

## Rules

- 先看市场和意图，再看单个 campaign。
- 不同市场的 CPA 不能脱离 AOV 和 ROI 比较。
- 低价市场可以保留学习预算，但不能用便宜注册掩盖付费弱。
