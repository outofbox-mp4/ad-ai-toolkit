# Example: Visual Ad Creative Brief

All product facts, offer details, and performance context below are fictional and supplied only to demonstrate the expected output.

## Input

- Product: FlowNote turns pasted meeting notes into assigned tasks and due dates
- Objective: Generate 14-day free-trial registrations
- Audience: Project leads at small teams who need clearer follow-up after meetings
- Funnel stage: Consideration
- Channels: Meta Reels and LinkedIn feed
- Approved proof: Create assigned tasks and due dates from pasted meeting notes
- Offer: 14-day free trial
- Restrictions: Do not claim guaranteed productivity gains or use customer testimonials

## Output

### Campaign proposition

Turn the uncertainty after a meeting into a clear list of owners and next steps.

### Creative matrix

| Angle | Audience tension | Hook | Proof | CTA | Test variable |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Meeting follow-up chaos | Notes, chat messages, and tasks are scattered after meetings | `The meeting ended. The follow-up chaos begins.` | Pasted notes become tasks, owners, and due dates | Start free trial | Pain-first opening |
| Notes are not action | Teams document meetings but do not turn them into work | `A meeting note is not a next step.` | Show note-to-task workflow | See how it works | Product-demo opening |
| Fewer status chases | Project leads repeatedly ask who owns the next step | `Stop asking: “Who owns this?”` | One view for tasks, owners, and due dates | Start free trial | Role-specific opening |

### Production brief: Meeting follow-up chaos

- **Hook:** A rapid sequence of fictional notes, chat reminders, and sticky notes fills the screen.
- **Visual direction:** At second 3, the clutter resolves into a FlowNote task view with `Task`, `Owner`, and `Due date` columns. Use generic, fictional task names only.
- **Primary text:** Meetings create decisions. FlowNote turns pasted notes into assigned next steps and due dates. Start a 14-day free trial.
- **Headline:** Turn Meeting Notes Into Next Steps
- **CTA:** Start Free Trial
- **Meta Reels adaptation:** Lead with the cluttered-screen contrast; use captions throughout.
- **LinkedIn feed adaptation:** Use a clean before-and-after carousel and a project-lead label.
- **Disallowed claims:** “Never miss a task”, quantified productivity gains, or customer testimonials.

### Test plan

- **Primary metric:** Trial registration CPA
- **Guardrails:** CTR and landing-page registration rate
- **Constant conditions:** Audience, budget, landing page, offer, and CTA
- **Learning window:** Run through at least one complete work week and wait for sufficient conversion volume
- **Decision rule:** Keep the winning angle by CPA; then test a new visual execution within that same angle
