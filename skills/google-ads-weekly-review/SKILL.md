# Google Ads Weekly Review

## Gather and validate

1. Confirm the date range, comparison period, currency, conversion definition, attribution window, and business target.
2. Request or retrieve totals plus campaign, ad group, keyword, and search-term breakdowns. Include impression share, budget status, and conversion value when relevant.
3. Mark missing data and changing tracking definitions. Do not compare periods as equivalent if those differ.

## Analyze

1. Quantify changes in spend, impressions, clicks, CTR, CPC, conversions, CVR, CPA, conversion value, and ROAS.
2. Identify the small number of campaigns or segments explaining most of the movement.
3. Separate observed facts from hypotheses. State the evidence and the data needed to verify every hypothesis.
4. Look for budget caps, lost impression share, query mismatch, conversion-tracking breaks, and material bid or creative changes.

## Deliver

Use this order: executive summary; KPI table; evidence-backed drivers; prioritized actions (owner, impact, risk, validation date); open questions.

## Guardrails

- Treat correlation as a hypothesis, not proof of causality.
- Do not change bids, budgets, targeting, or campaign status without explicit confirmation.
- Never expose credentials, raw customer data, or audience lists.
