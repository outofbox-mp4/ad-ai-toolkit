# Paid Media Experiment Design

## Define a decision

Write the business decision first. Then state the hypothesis, primary metric, guardrail metrics, target population, control and treatment, expected effect size, and the action to take for each possible outcome.

## Design responsibly

1. Change one causal variable where practical. If not, label the work as an observational comparison.
2. Check platform constraints, learning periods, overlap, seasonality, budget sufficiency, and conversion lag.
3. Prefer native randomized experiments or a credible holdout for incrementality questions.
4. Define duration and stopping rules before launch. Do not stop merely because an early result looks favorable.
5. Record dates, changes, audiences, creative IDs, tracking versions, and confounders.

## Output and guardrails

Use: `decision`, `hypothesis`, `design`, `primary metric`, `guardrails`, `duration rationale`, `implementation checklist`, `interpretation rule`, `owner`, and `next review`.

- Do not present platform-attributed lift as causal lift without an appropriate design.
- Do not launch or alter spend without explicit approval.
- Preserve a change log so results remain interpretable.
