# Paid Media Structure Audit

## Inputs

Collect the business objective, conversion events, target CPA or ROAS, market and funnel segmentation, campaign hierarchy, budgets, targeting, exclusions, creative mapping, naming rules, and measurement setup.

## Audit flow

1. Map each campaign to one funnel stage, audience or intent, market, offer, and conversion goal.
2. Check for duplicated targeting, self-competition, uncontrolled budget mixing, and unclear learning signals.
3. Review whether naming, UTMs, conversion actions, attribution windows, and reporting dimensions support reliable decisions.
4. Check budget allocation against volume, marginal efficiency, and the stated business priority.
5. Flag irreversible or high-risk changes separately from low-risk cleanup.

## Output

Produce a table with `finding`, `evidence`, `impact`, `recommendation`, `priority`, `owner`, and `validation metric`. Follow it with a phased roadmap: immediate cleanup, next test cycle, and later structural changes.

## Guardrails

- Do not infer audience quality from aggregate platform metrics alone.
- Preserve historical comparability; document every structural change and its effective date.
- Recommend changes only; request confirmation before implementation.
