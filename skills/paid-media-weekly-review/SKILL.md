# Paid Media Weekly Review

## Gather and validate

1. Confirm the platform or channel, date range, comparison period, currency, conversion definition, attribution window, and business target.
2. Request or retrieve account totals plus the relevant breakdowns: campaign, ad set or group, audience, creative, placement, keyword, search term, product, or market. Use only dimensions the platform provides.
3. Mark missing data, tracking changes, attribution differences, and cross-platform deduplication limits. Do not compare periods or platforms as equivalent when those differ.

## Analyze

1. Quantify available changes in spend, reach or impressions, clicks, CTR, CPC, conversions, CVR, CPA, conversion value, and ROAS.
2. Identify the small number of campaigns, audiences, creatives, queries, placements, or markets explaining most of the movement.
3. Separate observed facts from hypotheses. State the evidence and the data needed to verify every hypothesis.
4. Look for delivery constraints, budget caps, saturation, audience or query mismatch, conversion-tracking breaks, and material bid, creative, targeting, or landing-page changes.

## Deliver

Use this order: executive summary; KPI table; evidence-backed drivers; prioritized actions (owner, impact, risk, validation date); open questions and data-quality caveats.

## Guardrails

- Treat correlation as a hypothesis, not proof of causality.
- Do not compare platform-attributed conversion totals without explaining attribution and deduplication differences.
- Do not change bids, budgets, targeting, creative, or campaign status without explicit confirmation.
- Never expose credentials, raw customer data, or audience lists.
