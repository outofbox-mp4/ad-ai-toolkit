# Example: Search Ad Assets

All product details below are fictional and supplied only to demonstrate the expected format.

## Input

- Product: FlowNote, a tool that turns meeting notes into assigned tasks and due dates
- Market: US English
- Conversion: Start a 14-day free trial
- Keyword theme: meeting action items
- Trigger: A project lead wants to turn meeting notes into follow-up work quickly
- Approved proof: Create assigned tasks and due dates from pasted meeting notes
- Landing-page CTA: Start free trial

## Output

### Message map

| Keyword theme | Trigger | Proof | CTA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| meeting action items | Turn notes into follow-up work | Assign tasks and due dates from meeting notes | Start a free trial |

### Headlines

| Asset | Role | Combination note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Meeting Action Items | keyword | Pair with proof or CTA headline |
| Turn Notes Into Tasks | trigger | Pair with keyword headline |
| Assign Tasks & Due Dates | proof | Use where product proof is needed |
| Start Your Free Trial | CTA | Use with a proof-focused combination |
| FlowNote for Project Teams | brand | Optional brand context |

### Descriptions

| Asset | Role | Combination note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Turn pasted meeting notes into assigned tasks and due dates. | proof | Pair with keyword and trigger headlines |
| Keep the next step clear after every project meeting. Start a free trial. | trigger + CTA | Pair with proof headline |

### Display path and extension ideas

- Display path: `meeting-notes/tasks`
- Sitelink: `How It Works` → product walkthrough page
- Callout: `14-Day Free Trial`

### Checks before launch

- Confirm current platform character limits.
- Confirm the product can perform the stated task for the advertised input type.
- Confirm each extension destination exists and matches its label.
