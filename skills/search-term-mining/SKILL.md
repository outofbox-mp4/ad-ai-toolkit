# Search-Term Mining

## Prepare

Confirm query, match type, campaign, spend, clicks, conversions, conversion value, date range, and conversion definition. Normalize obvious spelling and casing variants without overwriting the original query.

## Classify

Assign each material query to: high-intent / retain or expand; research / route appropriately; competitor / apply policy; irrelevant / negative-keyword candidate; or ambiguous / manual review. Use commercial relevance, conversion evidence, volume, cost, and landing page—not a single metric.

## Recommend

1. Group high-intent queries into themes and propose campaign, ad-group, keyword, ad-copy, and landing-page implications.
2. Present negative candidates at query, phrase, and broad-theme levels; state false-positive risk.
3. Rank opportunities by likely impact and confidence.
4. Include a review queue for low-volume or ambiguous terms.

## Output

Return a summary plus: `theme`, `queries`, `spend`, `conversions`, `CPA/ROAS`, `recommended action`, `risk`, and `reviewer`.

## Guardrails

- Never add negatives automatically; show exact proposed terms and match levels for approval.
- Do not use sensitive personal attributes to infer intent or targeting.
